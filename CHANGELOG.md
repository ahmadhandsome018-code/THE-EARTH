THE EARTH — Changelog

All notable project changes are documented here.

[0.1.0] — Initial Project Foundation

Project Setup

- Created the initial Vite and React project configuration.
- Added the initial HTML entry point.
- Established the project structure for future development.
- Selected Three.js, React Three Fiber and Drei for 3D development.

World Design

- Defined the initial world concept.
- Established 12 starting cities.
- Defined 5,000,000 player-owned land squares.
- Clarified that player-owned land is not the total world area.
- Established the need for chunk streaming and LOD.

City Governance

- Defined the first successful entrant of each city as its first historical manager.
- Established the global Founders Badge for the first 1,000 registered players.
- Defined election cycles and voting requirements.
- Established city budgets and public infrastructure management.

Graphics

- Established Low, Medium, High and Ultra graphics settings.
- Made Ultra conditional on compatible hardware and graphics processing support.
- Defined photorealistic graphics as the Ultra quality target.
- Established local GPU processing and WebGL 2 / WebGPU support paths.

Languages and Devices

- Established Arabic and English as initial languages.
- Planned future multilingual support.
- Defined RTL and LTR interface support.
- Established desktop and mobile support.
- Defined the future 2D low-resource mode.

Economy

- Established PAL as the internal virtual currency.
- Defined the 3% tax on eligible PAL transactions for city treasuries.
- Separated PAL accounting from real USD payments.
- Cancelled the previous general 10% commercial and advertising tax.
- Established economic monitoring requirements.

Market Freedom and Protection

- Preserved free market pricing without fixed maximum prices.
- Allowed legitimate speculation and trading.
- Added market analytics and price anomaly detection.
- Added transaction monitoring and ownership tracking.
- Added resource monitoring and audit records.
- Established investigations and graduated penalties for proven manipulation.

Businesses and Production

- Defined player businesses, factories, agriculture and resource production.
- Established the Offerwalls and Clipping reward concepts.
- Defined the need for verified campaign rewards and fraud prevention.

Real Estate and Marketplace

- Defined land ownership, construction, upgrades and property transfers.
- Cancelled the black market.
- Established an official player account marketplace in real USD.
- Defined the 10% account marketplace commission.

Creators

- Established the Creator Program concept.
- Defined the 3,000 USD purchased-credit entry requirement.
- Established identity verification and separate accounting for PAL, purchased credits and cash earnings.

Life Simulation

- Defined health, food, sleep, weather and environmental systems.
- Established wildlife and ecological simulation.
- Defined sports and football activities.
- Established the five-life system and ten-day suspension rule.

Infrastructure and Technology

- Established the proposed Cloudflare backend architecture.
- Defined server-authoritative transactions and persistent records.
- Established the need for multiplayer communication and secure creator scripting.

Documentation

- Created the master project specification in PROJECT_SPEC.md.
- Established this changelog as the record of future project changes.
